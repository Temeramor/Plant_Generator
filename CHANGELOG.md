# Changelog

- Increased procedural tree heights and crown dimensions towards realistic mature sizes: oak (~18–28 m), birch (~15–24 m), horse chestnut (~17–27 m), poplar (~22–35 m), and spruce (~22–37 m).
- Adjusted branch-start heights and species-specific branch/foliage sizes while keeping individual seeded procedural variation.
- Generate distant LOD trees directly on a 0.2 m voxel grid rather than generating full-resolution trees and downsampling afterward.
- Expanded tree-grid bounds for secondary branches and foliage lobes; collision dimensions remain in world-scale units.
